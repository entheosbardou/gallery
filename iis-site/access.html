Access settings

Choose what each AD group can access. Membership is managed in AD under User Accounts → Services.

Category permissions from all matching groups are combined. An all-category viewing group or an administrator group grants broader access even when the same account is in an SFW-only group. Administrator access always includes all collections, every category and management tools.

Collection overrides apply to the selected collection and all its children. A more specific child override takes priority. Hide a parent, then Show a child to make an exception. Remove an override to inherit. Across matching viewing groups, the most specific override wins; Show wins a tie. Without an override, collections are visible if any viewing group shows them by default. Category restrictions still apply.
